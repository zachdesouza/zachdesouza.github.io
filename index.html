01 / My Story

A little about my story.

A little about where I come from, what I enjoy and the things that make me who I am.

Hi, I'm Zach.

I was born and raised in Malaysia, with family roots that trace back more than 500 years to Malacca. I'm of Malay and Portuguese descent, with ties to the Kristang community, also known locally as Orang Serani.

It's a heritage I'm proud of, and growing up surrounded by different cultures has shaped the way I see people and the world around me.

I've always loved travelling, but it's not necessarily the famous landmarks that make a trip memorable. Sometimes, it's a conversation with a local, a walk through a small market or discovering a place that's not on the usual tourist trail.

Those little experiences often tell you more about a place than any guidebook could.

Travelling has also taught me to stay curious. Every place has its own stories, traditions and way of life. There's always something new to discover, especially when you take the time to look beyond the obvious.

Outside of work, I'm the kind of person who gets drawn into new ideas. If something catches my interest, I can easily spend hours reading about it, experimenting or figuring out how it works.

I enjoy learning new skills, trying things I've never done before and occasionally getting carried away with a project just because I find it interesting.

Technology is another big interest of mine. I'm fascinated by how it continues to change the way we communicate, work and learn.

That curiosity has led me to explore digital platforms, website development, creative tools and, more recently, artificial intelligence.

On the professional side, I've spent over 20 years working across communications, public relations, marketing, branding, digital media and stakeholder engagement.

I've been involved in everything from corporate campaigns and events to developing communication strategies and digital projects.

Through it all, I've learned that good communication isn't just about what you say, but how well you connect with people.

This website is a little collection of who I am, what I do and what interests me.

You'll find bits of my professional journey, travels, creative projects and whatever else happens to catch my attention along the way.

Nothing too complicated. Just a place to share my experiences, ideas and a few stories of my own.

Thanks for stopping by.
