01 / My Story

A Little About My Story.

Hi, I'm Zach.

I was born and raised in Malaysia, and my family roots go back more than 500 years to Malacca. I come from a mixed family. My mum is Portuguese and my dad is Malay, so I'm Malay-Portuguese, with roots in the Kristang community, or Orang Serani as we're commonly known here.

I've always been proud of where I come from. Growing up with two different cultural backgrounds has given me a bit of both worlds, and I think that's partly why I've always been interested in different people, cultures and ways of life.

Travelling is something I've always enjoyed. Of course, I love visiting beautiful places, but sometimes it's the little things that make a trip memorable. Walking around a local market, trying food I've never heard of, chatting with someone I've just met or simply sitting somewhere and watching the world go by. Those are usually the moments I remember most.

I guess travelling has also made me more curious about the world. Every country has its own character, traditions and stories. I enjoy discovering those differences and experiencing places for myself rather than just reading about them or seeing them on social media.

When I'm not working or travelling, I'm usually finding something new to keep myself occupied. I'm the type of person who gets curious about something and ends up spending hours trying to figure it out. Sometimes it's a new skill, sometimes a creative idea, and sometimes it's just something random that caught my attention.

I'm also really into technology, especially new developments in artificial intelligence. I've always been fascinated by how quickly technology evolves and what we can actually do with it. AI, websites, digital tools, new apps, you name it. If something interesting comes along, chances are I'll want to try it. I enjoy experimenting, figuring things out and occasionally building something just to see whether I can make it work. I'm definitely not an expert in everything, but that's part of the fun. There's always something new to learn.

As for work, I've spent over 20 years in communications, public relations, marketing, branding and digital media. I've been involved in all sorts of things over the years, from campaigns and events to working with different people and organisations. It's been quite a journey, with plenty of experiences, challenges and lessons along the way. And even after all these years, there's still something new to learn.

So, why this website?

Well, I've always liked the idea of having my own little space on the internet. Somewhere I can share my travels, photographs, work experiences, random thoughts and whatever else I'm interested in at the time. Nothing too formal, and definitely not another corporate website.

I don't really have a particular theme or plan for everything I'll share here. Some days it might be about a place I've visited, other days something I've been working on, or maybe just a random thought that I feel like putting into words.

It's basically a little bit of everything that makes me, well, me.

Anyway, that's a little about myself.

Thanks for stopping by.
